|---|---|---|---|

| Liu et al., §3.4 and Fig. 5 | Same carbide model | 0.1 MPa per modeled hydrocarbon product (C₁–C₈), CO₂ and H₂O in the baseline | Independent gas chemical-potential inputs. Fig. 5e uses a different pressure experiment. The project leaves species-specific product pressures open. |
| Paalanen and Weckhuysen, §1, p. 4203 | Industrial Fe-based catalysts in both regimes; Co is also used for LTFT | LTFT 200–250 °C; HTFT 320–375 °C; reaction pressures 15–60 bar | Context across facilities. These are separate temperature windows. The review does not identify a unique active phase for this project. |
| IUPAC Gold Book, entry S05921 | Gas reference, independent of catalyst | Standard pressure 10⁵ Pa | Project adopts 1 bar for the standard chemical potential. |
| Project thermochemistry and molecular coverage contracts | State- and inventory-specific | Harmonic surface contributions, site-based molecular reference, explicit vacancies | Project model choices. Footprints, activities, site densities and validated state corrections require their own evidence. |

523 K corresponds to 249.85 °C, near the upper end of the cited LTFT window; the syngas feed corresponds to 25 bar. Neither comparison identifies an industrial active facet. The paper's distinction between Fe sites and carbon vacancies is specific to the carbide model. A carbon vacancy in a carbide lattice cannot supply the site inventory for metallic Fe(110).
