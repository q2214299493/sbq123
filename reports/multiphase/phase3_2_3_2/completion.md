CO at 0.83 MPa and H₂ at 1.67 MPa exhaust the syngas feed budget. Their ratio at published precision is about 2.012. Each product can have its own reservoir pressure. The paper's baseline assigns 0.1 MPa per product. The project leaves those pressures unspecified until a product model is chosen; a co-feed needs a defined total and a consistent composition.

The provisional ideal surface model uses one molecular activity on the species' declared site-based reference. For a multidentate species, its contribution to inventory *i* is the number of molecules times its footprint on *i*, divided by the size of *i*. Each independent inventory conserves occupied and vacant sites. Gas activities use the 1 bar standard pressure; an ideal-gas estimate uses the species partial pressure. An alternative gas, vibrational or surface activity model requires a stated source and domain. [Thermochemistry reference](thermochemistry_reference.json)

**Worked Fe(110) TS reference at 523 K.** The existing Grade A binding identifies 11 positive modes and excludes reaction-coordinate mode 12. Using the existing TS scale of 1.0 and retaining every positive mode gives ZPE 0.51405165 eV, thermal enthalpy increment 0.23455413 eV, and vibrational entropy 0.0008969835 eV/K. The resulting local vibrational Gibbs correction is **+0.27948341 eV per TS**. It is conditional on this harmonic subspace and comparison temperature.
