# Fe(110) applicability and outstanding evidence

**Scope.** CH* + CO* → CHCO* on metallic Fe(110) remains a mechanistic reference. The project now has a literature-grounded comparison temperature, feed and standard-state convention. The stable working catalyst and the abundance of its sites under these conditions remain unknown. Evaluating carbide surfaces and carbide/metal or carbide/oxide interfaces requires phase, stability and site evidence under the relevant gas chemical potentials.

| Item | What this phase supplies | What remains to use it for real Fe kinetics |
|---|---|---|
| Operating conditions | Provisional 523 K, 2.5 MPa syngas and nominal molar ratio of 2 | Scoped researcher review of applicability to the chosen catalyst and reactor. For product-dependent steps specify each reservoir or reactor composition, including any product co-feed and pressure balance. |
| Standard states | 1 bar gas; provisional dimensionless molecular site coverage and inventory-specific vacancy fraction | Define the molecular reference, species footprints and site counts for each surface or interface. Explicit nonlocal surface activities are required for cross-inventory rate evaluation. Absolute concentration needs site density; dimensionless reporting can proceed without it. |
| IS and FS thermochemistry | Independent target structures and electronic energies from the endpoint audit | For **each** state, supply either a structure-mapped signed spectrum or reviewed precomputed components at 523 K with comparable thermal basis, units and reference. A partial upload can advance one endpoint. The need for a new frequency job remains undetermined. |
| TS thermochemistry | Grade A spectrum gives a conditional local vibrational correction at 523 K; mode 12 remains excluded | Confirm the common physical basis with the two endpoints. The existing TS convention applies only to its reviewed local subspace. |
| Site and kinetic evidence | An activity convention to use once sites are assigned | Confirm adsorbate and vacancy stoichiometry, phase/domain assignments, charge balance as applicable, reaction reversibility, rate law and reviewed directional parameters. |
| Reaction free energy, barriers, rates and execution | Electronic barriers remain available in the historical audit | Reaction ΔG, forward/reverse ΔG‡ and rate constants remain unavailable. Fe MKM remains **blocked**. |

No product pressure, vacancy fraction, site density or endpoint correction is inferred from a missing value. Either endpoint may use a compatible external source without a local VASP job ID.
