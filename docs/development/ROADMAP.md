## V3.2 development strategy (temporary)

Complete the planned scientific functionality before the comprehensive independent audit.
Work through the following milestones in order.

1. Finish the Unified Trace-Species Numerical Integrity Correction.
2. Complete Scientific Analysis, then Scientific Interpretation.
3. Complete Mechanism Discovery and connect the full scientific workflow.
4. Demonstrate the workflow end to end on one real catalyst system. Identify
   which steps and outputs have qualified, which remain unqualified, and what
   evidence is missing. Record the limits of the demonstration.

During implementation, apply each method's requirements only to the operations
and systems that depend on them. Work within the existing directories, modules,
and interfaces. Defer a new System Profile, Capability Assessment, independent
adaptation layer, and broad architectural restructuring.

Record overly strict validation rules, interface limitations, and system
applicability issues as they arise, with the affected operation and evidence.
Prioritize confirmed scientific correctness defects. Track ordinary P2/P3
findings while continuing the main development sequence. Use focused checks
for each change and reserve full regression runs for integration or a concrete
cross-module risk. Carry the work forward in one integrated candidate.

Once the major functions and real-system demonstration are complete, hand the
single complete V3.2 candidate, its evidence, and its known limitations to
Gemini for one comprehensive independent audit. Use Gemini's actual findings
to decide whether to revise validation rules, adjust interfaces, or introduce
an adaptation layer.

At V3.2 development closeout, remove this temporary section and its backlog
pointer. Retain the demonstration evidence, qualification limits, and audit
findings with their respective records.
