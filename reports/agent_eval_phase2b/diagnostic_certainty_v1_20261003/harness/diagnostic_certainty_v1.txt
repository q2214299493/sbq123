Diagnostic certainty rule:

Separate the observed failure from its causal explanation.

Use root_cause_status="confirmed" only when the supplied evidence
directly establishes the cause itself.

A triggered guard, exceeded threshold, failed coverage check,
non-convergence condition, or other observed symptom confirms that
the condition occurred; it does not by itself confirm why it occurred.

If the evidence establishes the failure class but the causal mechanism
still requires diagnosis, use root_cause_status="hypothesis".

Do not infer model error from a geometry/path guard alone.
